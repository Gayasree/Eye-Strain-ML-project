💡 Eye Strain Prevention Tips

Simple habits to protect your eyes from digital strain.

⏱

20-20-20 Rule

Every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds to relax your eye muscles.

🌙

Enable Blue Light Filter

Use night mode or blue light filter apps on all devices, especially after sunset, to reduce melatonin disruption.

🔅

Adjust Screen Brightness

Match your screen brightness to the ambient light in your room. Avoid using screens in complete darkness.

😴

Get Enough Sleep

Aim for 7-9 hours of sleep per night. Sleep is when your eyes recover from daily strain.

👁

Blink More Often

People blink 66% less when staring at screens. Consciously blink to keep your eyes moist and prevent dryness.

👷

Maintain Proper Distance

Keep screens at arm's length (50-70 cm). Position the top of the screen at or slightly below eye level.

🌿

Stay Hydrated

Drink plenty of water throughout the day. Dehydration can worsen dry eye symptoms.

📅

Limit Total Screen Time

Try to keep recreational screen time under 2 hours per day outside of work/study requirements.
