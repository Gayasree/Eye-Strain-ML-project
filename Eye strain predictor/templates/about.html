About This Application

A simple and smart tool that helps you understand your risk of digital eye strain based on your everyday screen habits so you can take better care of your eyes.

🔍 What is Digital Eye Strain?

Digital Eye Strain is a very common condition that affects people who spend long hours looking at screens whether it's a phone, laptop, tablet, or TV. You may have experienced it without even realising: tired eyes, headaches, blurry vision, or dry and irritated eyes after a long day of screen use. In today's world, most of us are on screens for many hours every day, making this a growing concern for people of all ages. This tool helps you check your personal risk level quickly and easily, and gives you helpful tips to protect your eyes.

⚙ How It Works

  • Step 1 — Answer a Few Questions: Fill in a short form about your daily screen habits things like how many hours you spend on screens, how often you take breaks, and whether you experience any eye discomfort.
  • Step 2 — Get Your Risk Level: Based on your answers, the tool instantly tells you whether your eye strain risk is Low, Medium, or High.
  • Step 3 — Read Your Tips: Along with your result, you'll receive easy-to-follow tips tailored to your habits to help you reduce eye strain in your daily life.

🌟 Why This Tool Matters

Most people don't realise how much their screen habits affect their eye health until the discomfort becomes hard to ignore. This tool is designed to raise awareness and encourage small, simple changes like taking regular breaks, adjusting screen brightness, or improving your posture that can make a big difference over time. Whether you're a student, a working professional, or someone who enjoys screen time at home, this tool is for you.

⚠ Please Note: This tool is meant for awareness purposes only and is not a medical diagnosis. It is not a replacement for professional eye care advice. If you are experiencing ongoing or serious eye discomfort, we strongly encourage you to visit a qualified eye care specialist. Your eye health matters take it seriously!
